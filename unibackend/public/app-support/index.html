Frequently Asked Questions

Orders
Go to My Orders in your profile. Each order shows real-time status updates: Order Confirmed → Being Prepared → Out for Delivery → Delivered. You'll also receive push notifications at every stage.
Go to My Orders, select the order, and tap Cancel Order. Orders can only be cancelled before they are out for delivery. Refunds for prepaid orders are processed within 3–5 business days.
Go to Profile → Complaints, submit a complaint with your order number, and our team will resolve it within 24 hours. For urgent issues, email us at kareemhesham105@gmail.com.
Delivery
We currently deliver to Cairo and Fifth Settlement (Al-Tagamoa Al-Khames). Enter your address during checkout to confirm delivery availability in your area.
We deliver from 9 AM to 10 PM, Saturday through Thursday. Friday deliveries are available from 2 PM to 10 PM. Choose your preferred time slot during checkout.
Delivery is FREE for orders above 200 EGP. For orders below 200 EGP, a flat delivery fee of 25 EGP applies. During promotional periods, we may offer free delivery on all orders.
Payment
We accept: Cash on Delivery (COD), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard), Mobile Wallets (Vodafone Cash, Orange Money, Etisalat Cash), and the in-app CART Wallet. Online payments are powered by Paymob.
Yes. We use Paymob, Egypt's leading payment gateway, with bank-level TLS encryption. Your card details are never stored on our servers. All card transactions are protected by 3D Secure authentication.
Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days after approval. For card payments, it may take an additional 5–7 days to reflect in your account depending on your bank.
Account
On the login screen, tap Forgot Password?. Enter your registered email address and we'll send you a reset code. Follow the steps to create a new password.
You can delete your account from Profile → Settings → Delete Account inside the app, or visit our account deletion page. All personal data is permanently removed within 7 days.
Go to Profile → Settings → Biometric Login and toggle it on. You'll be prompted to enter your password once to authorize it. Biometric data never leaves your device.
