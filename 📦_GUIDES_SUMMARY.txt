💡 PRO TIPS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

    1. Test EVERYTHING before submitting
       → Rejection delays launch by days

    2. OAuth Consent Screen MUST be Published
       → Most common mistake!

    3. Create Android Client #2 AFTER uploading
       → You need Play Store's SHA-1

    4. Be patient with Google's review
       → First apps take 3-7 days

    5. Save your SHA-1 certificates
       → You'll need them for troubleshooting
